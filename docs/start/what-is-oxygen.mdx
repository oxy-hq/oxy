Connect your warehouse, ask questions in plain English, and Oxygen's agents
generate SQL, execute it, and stream back the answer — with charts, tables,
and follow-up questions in the same thread.

Oxygen is delivered as a hosted product at
[app.oxygen-hq.com](https://app.oxygen-hq.com). There is nothing to install.

## Five things Oxygen does

1. **Answers questions about your data** through a chat panel powered
   by your choice of LLM. ([Ask data questions →](/guide/build/ask-data-questions))
2. **Builds Procedures** — multi-step, replayable analytics
   workflows. ([Procedures →](/guide/build/procedures))
3. **Generates Data Apps** — dashboards composed of SQL queries,
   charts, tables, and controls. ([Data Apps →](/guide/build/data-apps))
4. **Models a Semantic Layer** so agents reason about your entities,
   not raw columns. ([Semantic Layer →](/guide/build/semantic-layer))
5. **Integrates everywhere your team works** — Slack, MCP clients,
   v0, Lovable, and any REST API consumer.
   ([Embed & extend →](/guide/build/embed-extend/mcp))

Oxygen Cloud is a managed service hosted at `app.oxygen-hq.com`: the web app
is where your team builds and explores, and the same platform runs every
agent, Procedure, and Data App for you behind its REST API (served under
`/api` on the same host).
