# Support & SLA <EnterpriseTag />

Standard Cloud customers have access to community support via
[Discord](https://discord.gg/m677N4EcRK) and email support through the
**Manage → Support** form.

[Enterprise customers](/guide/manage/enterprise) receive:

- SLA-backed response times
- A dedicated Slack channel
- Custom onboarding and quarterly reviews
- Direct line to the engineering team for production incidents
