Oxygen workspaces have three roles:

- **Owner** — full control of the workspace, including billing.
- **Admin** — can invite members, change settings, and edit any project.
- **Member** — can build agents, run Automations, and ask questions.

Manage members from **Manage → Members**. Bulk invites are supported.

Two rules protect the workspace: only an **Owner** can change another Owner or Admin,
and a workspace always keeps at least one Owner (transfer ownership before removing
the last one).

For the full picture — including **partner roles**, where one organization manages
another — see [Permissions](/manage/permissions).
