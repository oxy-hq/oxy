Workspaces choose how their team signs in. The default is
**Magic link** (passwordless email). Workspaces can also enable
**Google OAuth** for one-click social sign-in, or — on the
[Enterprise tier](/manage/enterprise) — **Okta SSO** with their
corporate identity provider.

## Multiple methods

You can enable multiple sign-in methods on a workspace at once. Users
see all enabled options on the sign-in screen.

Method preferences are managed from **Manage → Authentication**.
